# AgentCorp Internal Company / Empresa Interna AgentCorp

Private general-purpose AI organization supporting strategy, research, engineering, auditing, marketing, sales and administration.

## Governance / Governança
- Human owner approves production deployments, external communications, purchases, transfers, contracts, credentials and destructive changes.
- Audit reports directly to CEO and remains independent from engineering.
- Workflow: intake, research, feasibility, owner approval, execution, independent audit, release approval, measure.
- Document sources, uncertainties, budget and outcomes.
- Language: pt-BR or English, following organization preference.
- Importing a team must not automatically start agents or grant credentials.
